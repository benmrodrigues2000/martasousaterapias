Common questions

What people often ask

What is Reiki and how does it work?
Reiki is a gentle energy-harmonising technique, given through hand placement. It's a calm, pain-free experience of deep relaxation — you remain fully clothed, in complete comfort.
Do I need to believe in something?
No. Sessions adapt to each person. Simply come with an open mind and a good disposition — the rest is about caring for the present moment.
Can I book a distance session?
Yes. I offer distance Reiki for those who can't attend in person or prefer the comfort of their own space, by appointment.
