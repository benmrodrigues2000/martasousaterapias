Preparing for your session

  • Wear comfortable, light, non-restrictive clothes
  • Arrive five minutes early
  • Avoid heavy meals shortly before
  • If it's in the afternoon, have a small glass of water before you leave

Self-care at home

  • Reserve five minutes a day just to breathe
  • Writing down what brings you peace helps you remember it
  • Care for your sleep — balance starts here
  • After a session, don't fill your schedule right away: let it settle
