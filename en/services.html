Services

Reiki & Angel Tarot Sessions

Every session is unique and tailored to you. If you're unsure which one is right for you, get in touch before booking.

01

60 minutes · In person

Reiki — individual session

Reiki is a gentle energy-harmonising technique, given through hand placement. In my space, it's a deep-relaxation experience, ideal for moments of stress, fatigue or imbalance — or simply for anyone who needs to stop and care for themselves.

  • A short opening conversation to understand what brings you in
  • A calm session in a peaceful room, fully clothed and in complete comfort
  • Finishes with simple self-care guidance for home
02

45 minutes · At a distance

Distance Reiki

For those who can't attend in person, who live far away, or who simply prefer the comfort of their own space. The session is performed at a distance, with the same intention and presence — from my space to yours, on an agreed day and time.

  • By appointment, on the day and time we agree
  • Best experienced in a quiet place where you can rest
  • Ideal for travellers or anyone with reduced mobility
03

50 minutes · In person or at a distance

Angel Tarot

Angel Tarot is a tool for reflection and personal guidance, based on the messages of the Archangels. It is not fortune-telling — it's a space to look at the present moment, clarify paths and find serenity in decisions.

  • A respectful, non-judgemental reflective reading
  • Focused on guidance for your current life moment
  • Full confidentiality
04

90 minutes · In person

Reiki + Angel Tarot

A complete journey in my space: first the Angel Tarot reading, to clarify what needs tending; then a Reiki session to integrate and harmonise. The choice of those who want to look within and let the body follow in peace.

  • Angel Tarot reading + Reiki session
  • The most complete, in-depth experience
  • Personalised self-care guidance
